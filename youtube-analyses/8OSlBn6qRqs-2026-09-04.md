## Analysis
- Strong educational structure: symptom cluster → mechanism → risk factors → dietary sources.
- Claims are broadly consistent with known thiamine deficiency manifestations, especially in severe deficiency/beriberi spectrum.
- Causal breadth is overstated for general population: B1 deficiency is not the #1 cause of ADHD, OCD, or psychosis in most people.
- Missing nuance: dose-response, prevalence data, differential diagnosis, and confounding factors are not addressed.
- Practical takeaway is still useful: refined carb reduction and B1 awareness can help some patients with neuropathic symptoms.
