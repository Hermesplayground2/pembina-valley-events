# Stock Movement Profiles

## SPCX — SpaceX
- **Type:** IPO/newly public high-beta tech/defense/space
- **Typical daily move:** Often 3-10% on news; can gap on earnings or lockup tranches
- **Key drivers:**
  1. **Earnings / revenue reports** — first public earnings were Aug 4, 2026; revenue beat caused +9%+
  2. **Lockup unlocks** — staggered schedule from Aug 6, 2026 through mid-2027; first tranche ~$123B shares free to sell
  3. **Launch outcomes** — Falcon 9 / Starship success or failure
  4. **Musk commentary / SEC / policy** — tweets, government contracts, Starship milestones
  5. **Analyst initiations / price targets** — limited history, so coverage moves stock
- **What to watch:** Volume spikes on lockup dates; pre-earnings positioning; any Starship failure/success

## RUM — RUM Group (formerly Rumble)
- **Type:** Small-cap media/AI infrastructure hybrid
- **Typical daily move:** 5-15% on catalysts; lower liquidity = bigger % swings
- **Key drivers:**
  1. **AI infrastructure news** — GPU sales, Northern Data integration, Quake AI updates
  2. **Political/content cycles** — election-year traffic boosts ad revenue narrative
  3. **Earnings** — last reported Nov 12, 2024; +/- 15.56% typical reaction; Q1 2026 miss caused -29.9%
  4. **Dilution / capital raises** — share issuance fears hit hard
  5. **Platform traffic / user metrics** — MAU/DAU spikes from controversy or political events
- **What to watch:** Any Northern Data/GPU deal update; earnings date announcements; unusual options activity; share count changes

## SLV — iShares Silver Trust
- **Type:** Commodity ETF tracking spot silver
- **Typical daily move:** 1-3% normally; 5%+ on macro shocks
- **Key drivers:**
  1. **Fed policy / real rates** — higher rates = pressure; dovish pivot = rally
  2. **Dollar strength (DXY)** — strong dollar smashes silver
  3. **China supply/export policy** — export bans or stockpile changes move it sharply
  4. **Industrial demand** — solar/EV production data, green transition policy
  5. **Inflation expectations / CPI** — reflation trade lifts silver
  6. **Gold/silver ratio shifts** — precious metals rotation
- **What to watch:** FOMC meetings, CPI/PCE releases, China trade/news, DXY spikes, options expiries
