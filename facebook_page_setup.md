Pembina Valley Events is your go-to source for what's happening across Manitoba's Pembina Valley. We highlight local events, activities, festivals, and community gatherings in Winkler, Morden, and Altona.

Whether you're looking for family-friendly activities, local food deals, church events, garage sales, or regional festivals like the Morden Corn & Apple Festival and the Winkler Harvest Festival, we've got you covered.
